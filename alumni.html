In Their Words

What they remember, years later.

“Nobody let me skip morning practice just because exams were close. I resented that at fifteen. I'm grateful for it now — it's basically my whole career.”

Rohan Vyas

Class of 2014 · Sports Physiotherapist, former state-ranked swimmer

“The robotics lab stayed open most evenings, and a teacher usually stuck around just to keep an eye on things. That quiet permission to stay and build stuff shaped what I do for a living now.”

Ishaan Pandya

Class of 2019 · Software Engineer

“I made my first short film for a Grade 10 assignment with a borrowed camera and three classmates. The teacher who graded it sternly still remembers the title. That's the kind of school this was.”

Aditi Bose

Class of 2008 · Documentary Filmmaker

“It wasn't only the toppers who got attention here. I was a solid-but-unspectacular student, and I still had three teachers who knew exactly what I was good at. That stuck with me.”

Farhan Sheikh

Class of 2013 · Chartered Accountant
